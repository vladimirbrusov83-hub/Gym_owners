Who this is for

You own or manage a gym. You have staff - coaches, front desk, maybe an office manager. You are not a developer, you have never written code, and you do not intend to start. You are busy, you are practical, and you have heard enough about AI to be curious but not convinced.

This course is for you.

Every tool here lives inside Claude. There is no software to install, no accounts to set up beyond Claude itself, and no one to call when something breaks. If you can write an email, you can build everything in this course.

What makes this different

Most AI courses for gym owners point you at automation tools - chatbots, CRMs, lead-follow-up sequences. Those tools solve the mechanical layer. This course solves the human layer: the intake conversation, the hard message, the communication that falls through the cracks because nobody had the right words.

Claude is not like other AI tools. It follows nuanced instructions, maintains context across a conversation, and produces output that sounds like a specific person at a specific gym - not a generic response. That is what this course teaches you to use.

Each module gives you a complete, ready-to-use prompt you can paste into Claude today. No setup. No waiting. No dependencies on a third-party platform that charges you monthly to use it.

Built by a strength and conditioning coach with 20+ years of experience in the gym - and someone who has spent a lot of time with Claude. This course is free. There is no email list, no upsell, and no follow-up sequence. Just the tools.
